The Problem

Growing businesses eventually outgrow manual processes.

The signs are familiar — spreadsheets with tabs nobody remembers the purpose of, decisions made in group chats, reports rebuilt by hand every Monday morning.

Paper-based workflows

Approvals still move on printed forms, signed by hand and filed in a drawer someone has to remember exists.

Spreadsheet overload

One workbook, twenty-two tabs, and three formulas nobody's allowed to touch becomes the system of record.

Chat-based coordination

Critical handoffs happen in group chats that scroll away, with no record of who agreed to what.

Manual reporting

Someone quietly rebuilds the same report by hand, every single week, copying numbers between tools.
